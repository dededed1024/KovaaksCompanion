# Benchmark rank calculation notes

The overall tier of a playlist follows evxl.app's Benchmark Builder exactly (ported from its JS bundle, `Zn` in
`/_app/immutable/chunks/`):

- **Complete tier**: the lowest scenario tier. Zero if any scenario is unplayed or unranked. Shown as "<Tier> Complete".
- **Custom tier**: the playlist's `rankCalculation` method (`playlists.json`, `RankCalculation.cs`).
- **Overall tier** = the higher of the two (`selectable-top-n` always uses the custom tier).

Progress toward the next tier always comes from the custom method, whatever the complete tier is. Methods without one
(`complete`) use the share of scenarios above the tier.

`playlists.json` is generated by `scripts/convert-benchmarks.mjs` from evxl's playlist data (`rankCalculation`,
`scenarioSelection` included). Regenerate it when evxl adds playlists.

The port was checked against evxl's own code on 3752 generated score sets over every playlist: tier and progress match
for all methods.
